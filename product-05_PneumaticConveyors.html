Pneumatic Conveyors

Ultra compact and efficient unit fits in where others won’t. Features and easily maintained cartridge filter system with a reverse air pulse system to maintain efficient, continuous operation. Can be easily integrated with processing equipment and into just about any plant layout.

Key Features

  • ▸ Integral blower eliminates the need for external compressors.
  • ▸ Reverse-pulse jet filter cleaning system improves filter efficiency.
  • ▸ Compact design allows easy in-plant installation.
  • ▸ Fully enclosed system ensures clean and dust-free operation.
  • ▸ High conveying efficiency with regenerative blower technology.
  • ▸ Easy cleaning when changing materials.

Advantages

Pneumatic conveyors reduce operating costs by eliminating the need for plant air and separate compressors. Their efficient filter cleaning system extends filter life, reducing maintenance and downtime.

Special Features

  • ▸ Reverse-pulse filter cleaning ensures no material buildup.
  • ▸ Side-access door for quick and easy filter maintenance.
  • ▸ Fully discharges material to avoid contamination.
  • ▸ Available with multiple filter options for different applications.

Customization & Options

Available in various configurations including portable units. Designed to meet specific plant requirements with flexible engineering and installation options.

Applications

Used in industries such as food processing, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and manufacturing for efficient and hygienic bulk material transport.

Industries We Serve

Food, Chemical, Pharmaceutical, Packaging, Manufacturing
