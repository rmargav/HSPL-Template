Flexible Screw Conveyor

Ideal for transferring finely divided, free-flowing bulk materials. Features simple maintenance with only one moving part. Available in carbon/stainless steel and polyethylene construction.

Key Features

  • ▸ Heavy-duty augers ensure smooth material movement without stalling.
  • ▸ Simple design reduces maintenance and operating costs.
  • ▸ Fully enclosed system minimizes product loss and contamination.
  • ▸ Can operate in tight and complex spaces with flexible routing.
  • ▸ Only one moving part for easy maintenance.
  • ▸ Quick disassembly within 20 minutes for cleaning.

Advantages

Flexible screw conveyors are one of the most economical solutions for material handling. With lengths up to 24.4 meters, they provide maximum efficiency at minimal cost.

Customization & Options

Available in various auger styles and sizes ranging from 5 cm to 20.3 cm diameter. Designed specifically for your application to ensure optimal performance.

Applications

Suitable for industries requiring efficient bulk material handling including food processing, chemicals, and manufacturing industries.

Food, Chemical, Pharmaceutical, Packaging, Manufacturing
